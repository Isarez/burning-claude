Burning Claude

A menu bar meter for your Claude usage — the 5-hour and 7-day limits, per account, read from Anthropic's own figures.

This installs Burning Claude.app into /Applications and starts it. It runs in the menu bar only: no Dock icon and no window, so look for the flame at the top right of your screen.

If a previous copy is running, it will be quit first. Your settings, tracked accounts and usage history are kept.
